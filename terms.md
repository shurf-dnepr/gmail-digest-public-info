# Terms of use

Updated: 30 October 2026

This application is a personal automation for its owner, not a public email service. Only accounts owned by the operator may be connected.

Summaries are generated automatically and may omit or misinterpret details. Consult the original email before taking important action. Scheduled delivery may be delayed or interrupted by provider outages, free-tier limits or revoked access.

The application does not promise uninterrupted delivery or permanently available third-party free quotas. It has no paid API fallback. Use of Gmail, GitHub, Gemini and Telegram remains subject to each provider's terms.
